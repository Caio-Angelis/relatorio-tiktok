TikTok Content Performance Analytics

Relatorio TikTok is a tool designed to help TikTok creators analyze the performance of their own content. Users can securely connect their TikTok account and allow the application to retrieve authorized profile and video information.

Explore the service ↓ Read the Privacy Policy

What it does

See the signals behind every post.

The application may analyze available metrics such as video views, likes, comments, shares, video metadata, and publication dates. The purpose of the application is to help creators understand which types of content perform better and improve their content strategy.
