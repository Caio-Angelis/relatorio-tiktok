Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Relatorio TikTok handles information accessed when you connect your TikTok account to the service.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the permissions and scopes you grant, Relatorio TikTok may access information made available through the TikTok API, including:

  • Basic account and profile information
  • Video information
  • Video metadata
  • View counts
  • Like counts
  • Comment counts
  • Share counts

The application only accesses information explicitly authorized by the TikTok user. Authentication is completed through TikTok's own authorization/OAuth flow. Relatorio TikTok does not collect or store TikTok passwords.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the TikTok API is used only to:

  • Display analytics
  • Track content performance
  • Compare video performance
  • Generate reports and insights for the user

Relatorio TikTok does not use the information to sell personal data or for purposes unrelated to the service described here.

3. TikTok API

Access to TikTok information depends on the user's explicit authorization and on the permissions or scopes granted by TikTok. TikTok may change, limit, or remove API access and available data at any time. The service only requests and uses information made available through the applicable authorization.

4. Data Sharing

Relatorio TikTok does not sell personal data. Information is used for the purposes described in this policy and may be transmitted to TikTok as necessary to complete authorization and API requests. We do not share personal data for unrelated advertising or data-broker purposes.

5. Data Storage

Where information is stored in connection with the service, reasonable measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No specific storage method or security measure is promised by this policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

7. Revoking TikTok Access

Users can revoke the application's access through their TikTok account settings when that option is available. Revoking access can prevent future API access, but it may not automatically delete information already associated with the service. To request deletion, use the contact information in this policy.
